TheWallMuseByYUNUS Pin Scheduler

A private tool of the Etsy shop TheWallMuseByYUNUS that schedules Pins for the shop's own digital wall art and printable products on the shop's own Pinterest account.

Privacy Policy

App: TheWallMuseByYUNUS Pin Scheduler  |  Last updated: November 15, 2026

What this app is

TheWallMuseByYUNUS Pin Scheduler is a private tool used by a single person, the owner of the Etsy shop TheWallMuseByYUNUS, to publish Pins for the owner's own digital products to the owner's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

With the owner's permission (OAuth), the app can read the owner's Pinterest boards and create Pins on those boards. The Pins contain a product image, a title, a description and a link to the product listing on Etsy.

Data we collect and store

We do not collect personal data from visitors or customers. The only data stored is the owner's Pinterest access token, which is kept in an encrypted secret store and used only to create the owner's own Pins. We do not collect data from other Pinterest users.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent or disclose any data to third parties. Data obtained through the Pinterest API is not used for advertising, profiling, or training of machine learning models.

Retention and deletion

The access token is deleted when the owner revokes the app's access in Pinterest settings or removes the app. The owner can revoke access at any time from Pinterest account settings under Apps.
